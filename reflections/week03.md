# Week 3 Reflection

Reflect 3: What does “authenticity” mean when identity is co-constructed with AI? When identity is being co-constructed with AI, the authenticity is not the same as just coming from yourself. When you are being authentic you are being true to yourself, and is coming from you. When AI is used to help portray your identity, you have to give the AI direction on what to know about your identity. This is where AI takes away the authenticity, because AI will create assumptions within where you may not be as specific. You are the one who can input the information you want AI to show your identity as, which can leave gaps for the AI to make assumptions. Although that doesn't mean you should tell AI to create a version of your identity you want to be perceived as, and not your true identity.
